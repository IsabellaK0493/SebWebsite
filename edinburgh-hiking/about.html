About your guide

Twenty years on Edinburgh's hills, and still out most weeks

I'm Sebastian. I was born and raised here, and I've been walking these hills since before I can properly remember.

How this started

I've been walking Edinburgh's hills from a very young age, and I still get out on them regularly — usually a mixture of trail runs and long walks with my two chocolate Labradors, Daisy and Poppy.

People kept asking me to take them up Arthur's Seat, or out to the Pentlands, or to show them somewhere green that wasn't full of other visitors. Enough of them asked that I started doing it properly.

What I do outside Edinburgh

I walk, run and explore the outdoors in the Highlands and abroad. I've completed a number of trail races, walks with a running club, the Arrochar Alps, the Glenshee 9, and a UTMB in Switzerland.

None of that is the point of these tours — but it does mean I'm comfortable on rough ground in bad weather, and I know when a day isn't worth pushing.

How the tours work

Every tour is private. That means either one-to-one, or a small group you organise yourself — there will be no strangers, and you can walk at your own pace. I plan the route around who is coming and what the weather is doing, and I always have a lower-level alternative in mind before we set off.

The short version
  • Born and raised in Edinburgh
  • 20+ years on the Pentlands and the seven hills
  • Private tours only — no mixed groups
  • All abilities, from flat and short to a full hill day
  • Two chocolate Labradors, occasionally along for the tour
