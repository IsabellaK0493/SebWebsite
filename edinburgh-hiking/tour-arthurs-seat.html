Arthur's Seat & Salisbury Crags

The hill that gives Edinburgh its shape. I have walked and run this ground hundreds of times and know every path up it, including the ones most visitors never find.

251 mHigh point
~2 kmFrom the centre
Half dayDuration
ModerateGrade

An extinct volcano in the middle of a capital city

Having lived in Edinburgh for most of my life, Arthur's Seat and Salisbury Crags are very familiar to me. I have gone for hundreds of walks and runs through the area, exploring all that the area has to offer and taking almost every possible path and route.

Alongside this, I have participated in a number of EMF 10km races and Parkruns around Arthur's Seat.

For being so centrally located, Arthur's Seat and Salisbury Crags offer a good choice of trails. There are more gentle walks around the peaks passing St Margaret's Loch and St Anthony's Chapel, to more inclined routes that take you to the edge of the Crags and to the top of the Arthur's Seat.

One of the real highlights of Arthur's Seat and the Crags are the views across Edinburgh you get after climbing them which, even after all these years, never get old for me.

I look forward to guiding you through this iconic area.
