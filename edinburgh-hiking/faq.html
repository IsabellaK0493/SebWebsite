Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually want to know before booking. If your question is not here, send it over and I will answer it.

Every tour is private, whether it is a solo or group tour. That means either one-to-one or a small group you organise yourself: there will be no strangers and you can walk at your own pace. I also plan the route around who is coming and what the weather is like, and we can always adjust it during the tour if anything changes.

On solo tours, you can bring another person with you free of charge. On group tours, every adult in attendance must pay.

Yes, you can bring your dog with you on any of the tours, provided they are well trained, you have them under control and can take responsibility for them throughout the whole tour. Do note, there are sheep on the Pentland Hills, so you must keep your dog on the lead or have them under close control, as it is a criminal offence to allow your dog to chase sheep.

Yes, children are allowed. Children aged 12 or under can join a group tour for free as long as they are with a paying adult. Children aged 13 to 16 can join a group for half the price. Anyone aged 17 or over pays full price. Do bear in mind that half or full day tours may not be suitable for younger children, given the physical demand of walking uphill and being on your feet for several hours.

No, you do not need experience, as I will guide you through the area. You will need a basic level of fitness and the ability to walk uphill and downhill on uneven terrain.

The difficulty depends on who joins the tour, as I set the pace and route based on the abilities of the group, and on the route you choose. For example, the quarter day tours for any of the Edinburgh hills are fairly accessible to anyone, as they are only 2 hours and do not involve a considerable amount of walking uphill. The Pentlands are a little more strenuous due to far more uphill sections, particularly the full day 8-hour tour.

No, transport is not included. However, all the hills in Edinburgh are easily accessible by bus or taxi, and the Pentland Hills has several buses that run regularly to the area. If you want any guidance on the buses, do get in touch.

No. You are responsible for wearing suitable footwear and clothing. Please check the weather forecast ahead of coming to the tour to help inform your decision on what to wear. For example, if rain is forecast, you will need waterproofs. If you have any questions, you can drop me a message.

For solo tours, a litre of water is provided. For both solo and group tours, I will bring some snacks with me. However, particularly for full day tours, you may wish to bring additional food to keep yourself energised throughout.

  • All tours must be paid for in advance, and tours must be used within 60 days of payment, unless otherwise agreed.
  • You must give me 48 hours notice to cancel or rearrange a tour, otherwise that tour will be lost without refund. You can only rearrange a tour once.
  • Tours are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Safety is my top priority, so if dangerous weather conditions are forecast (for example weather warnings) ahead of us starting the tour, I will make the call to reschedule to another day at no additional cost, even if we are within the 48-hour window.
  • I am happy to hike in any weather: rain, shine, or wind. However, you are welcome to reschedule your tour on one occasion for any reason, including the weather forecast, at no extra cost if done more than 48 hours before your tour start time.
  • Within 48 hours, I do not allow rescheduling or cancellations, even if the forecast looks unpleasant.
  • In the event of bad weather during the tour, our original route may have to be shortened, altered or cancelled for safety reasons.

Still unsure about something? Get in touch and I will come back to you, usually within 48 hours.
