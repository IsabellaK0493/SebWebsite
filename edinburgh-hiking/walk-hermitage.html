Hermitage of Braid & Blackford Hill

A burn, a wooded glen and a short climb onto Blackford Hill — a local nature reserve almost entirely inside the city, and the best close view of the castle there is.

164 mHigh point
~4 kmFrom the centre
Half dayDuration
EasyGrade

The gentlest walk I run

I have been visiting this local nature reserve since I was around five years old. Back then we would collect sticks, explore the woodland areas with our family chocolate Labrador, and sledge the slopes when it snowed.

It is the easiest of the three walks and the one I would suggest if you want somewhere green and quiet without leaving Edinburgh, or if you are walking with children or anyone less sure on steep ground.

The reserve feels a long way from the city, right up until you reach the top of Blackford Hill and the whole skyline appears at once — the castle, Arthur's Seat, the Forth behind.

On the day

Start

Meet at the gate

We start at the entrance to the Hermitage and follow the Braid Burn into the glen.

1

Through the glen

Level walking under old trees, with the burn alongside and the walled garden and ice house on the way.

2

Up Blackford

A short, steady climb out of the trees onto the open hill — the only real ascent of the day.

3

The view

The Royal Observatory sits just below the summit; the view north takes in most of the city in one go.

End

Back down

A loop back through the reserve rather than retracing the same path.
