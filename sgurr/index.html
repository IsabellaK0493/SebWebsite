Who we are

Guiding Scotland's hills since 2011, out of a shop on the Grassmarket.

Sgùrr was founded by two Winter Mountain Leaders who kept getting asked the same question at the end of a route: which one's next? We built a company around answering it properly — matching the right mountain to the right person, in the right conditions, with someone qualified to bring them back down again.

We're based in Edinburgh, an hour from the Southern Highlands and three from Torridon, which means most of our clients start the day from their own front door or a city-centre hotel, not a bunkhouse car park.

  • WML Every lead guide holds Winter Mountain Leader or International Mountain Leader qualification, current for winter conditions, navigation and rope work.
  • 1:4 Maximum client-to-guide ratio on exposed ridge routes, tightened further above the snowline.
  • Mountaineering Scotland Members and route-registered, with full Scottish Mountain Rescue callout cover on every route we run.
  • Weather-led Every itinerary carries a foul-weather alternative decided the night before, not improvised at the car park.
The Compleat Path

A guided route through all 282, planned around your weekends.

Compleating the Munros — climbing all 282 — usually takes a working hillwalker a decade of scattered weekends and a few wasted trips to hills that were never in condition. The Compleat Path is our standing programme for it: a multi-year plan built around your calendar, grouping summits by region and season, with priority booking on the hills that are hardest to catch in good weather.
