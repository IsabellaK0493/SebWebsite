Founded 2011 · Edinburgh

Two guides, a spare room above a bootfitter's, and a waiting list within a year.

Sgùrr started as a favour between friends and became the way we make a living. Here's the version of the story that's actually true.

The Grassmarket, 2011

Iain MacRae had been guiding informally for years — taking colleagues, then colleagues' friends, up Munros on weekends off from a proper mountaineering job. Freya Lindqvist, then ski patrol at Cairngorm, kept getting the same calls: could she just take someone up Ben Nevis, safely, without the faff of joining a club. In 2011 they put a name on it and rented a single room above a bootfitter's on the Grassmarket. Within a year they had a waiting list for winter dates.

Callum Ross joined in 2016 to build out the scrambling and Cuillin programme — routes the other two were qualified for but didn't have the bandwidth to run properly alongside everything else. Fifteen years on, we're still small on purpose: three lead guides, a short bench of freelance Mountain Leaders for busy weekends, and a rule that no guide runs more than four days back-to-back without a rest day.

What "properly" means to us

  • Qualified Every lead guide holds Winter Mountain Leader or International Mountain Leader qualification, kept current every year, not renewed once and forgotten.
  • Small We turn down group bookings above eight people rather than dilute the ratio on exposed ground.
  • Honest If a route isn't right for you on the day — fitness, weather, nerve — we say so and offer the alternative, rather than push on and hope.
  • Local Every guide lives within an hour of the hills they lead on. We're not flying in for the season.
The team

Who you'll actually be roped to.

IM

Iain MacRae

Founder & Lead Guide · WML

Raised on Skye, qualified Winter Mountain Leader since 2006. Has compleated the full round of Munros twice — the second time linking trailheads by bike. Leads our winter mountaineering and Cuillin programme, and still personally plans every Compleat Path itinerary in his first year with a client.

FL

Freya Lindqvist

Chief Guide · IML

International Mountain Leader, formerly ski patrol at Cairngorm Mountain. Handles route planning and weather calls for the whole guide team — if a route is cancelled the morning of, it was almost certainly her decision, and it was almost certainly right.

CR

Callum Ross

Expedition Lead · ML, RCI

Mountain Leader and Rock Climbing Instructor. Joined in 2016 to build the scrambling and ridge programme and hasn't stopped adding routes since — Aonach Eagach, An Teallach, and most of our Skye expeditions are his design.
