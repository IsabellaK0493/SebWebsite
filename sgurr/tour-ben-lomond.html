Ben Lomond

Beinn Laomainn — the most southerly Munro, and for most of our clients, the first one. A clear path, one steep pull below the summit ridge, and views the length of the loch on a clear day.

974mSummit height
720mAscent
11kmRound trip
6–7hrsTypical duration

Why we start people here

Ben Lomond sits directly above the loch, which means the path is well built, the navigation is straightforward in good visibility, and the summit ridge gives a genuine, exposed-feeling finish without technical difficulty. It's the Munro we recommend when someone asks "am I ready for this" — by the top, they usually know the answer.

Grid reference NN 367 129 marks the summit trig point. We log the exact ascent and conditions for every client who wants it added to their own Munro ledger.

The route

07:45

Pickup, Edinburgh

Collection from central Edinburgh or direct meet at the Rowardennan car park, on Loch Lomond's eastern shore.

09:15

Ptarmigan ridge ascent

A steady climb through birch and oak woodland, opening onto the Ptarmigan ridge with the first clear views over the loch and its islands.

12:00

Summit ridge

The final approach narrows along a rocky crest with drops to either side — the one section of the day that feels like real mountain terrain.

13:30

Descent via the tourist path

A more direct return, giving you a second route down and a look at the hill from a different angle.
