My story

Hi, I'm Seb.

I'm a Level 3 certified personal trainer who helps people get stronger and more flexible. I also hold a Psychology degree from the University of St Andrews, which shapes how I coach: the physical and the mental side of training go together, and confidence in the gym matters as much as the weight on the bar.

I've been an athlete all my life — track and hill running, rugby and calisthenics. Training several physical qualities at once, and competing on top of it, taught me first-hand what a body needs to perform. That's what lets me build programmes that fit the person in front of me.

From national-level runners to students, executives and busy parents.

Most of the people I work with want to build strength and flexibility. Others want to run faster, build muscle, or finally feel at home in the free-weights area. I coach in person at PureGym Quartermile in Edinburgh, and online with clients around the world.

What I help with

Three things, done properly.

Every programme is built around your goal. These are the ones I help with most — and the kind of wins clients have had.

Flexibility

Get more flexible and mobile — for everyday comfort, for sport, or to move without the stiffness that's crept in.

  • Touching toes for the first time
  • Reaching the front splits
  • A better front-rack position

Running

Strength work that makes you a faster, more resilient runner — from your first 5k to hill races and marathons.

  • A sub-20-minute 5km
  • Getting back to running after injury
  • A first half-marathon

Strength

Get stronger and able to do more — with technique that keeps you training for years, not weeks.

  • Squatting your bodyweight
  • Your first full chin-up
  • Ten full push-ups

Gym confidence

New to the gym or self-conscious around weights? We'll get you using the free-weights area on your own, with a plan you understand.

Competitions & challenges

From a first Hyrox to a week-long walk in the Pyrenees — preparation built around the date that matters.
